Our story

Three generations.
One marinade.

  1. Grandma

    Created the original homemade teriyaki marinade for meat. The secret starts here.

  2. Dad

    Refined the family recipe over the years, adding his own personal touch.

  3. Garrett

    Carried on the tradition — and created reduced-sodium and reduced-sugar versions so more people can enjoy it.

It actually started with backpacking. Garrett was dehydrating fruit for the trail when someone said, “Why don't you try making beef jerky?” So he did. Friends wanted some. Then their friends. A simple hobby turned into GLOWZ Jerky.

Made in Hawaiʻi with aloha! 🤙
— Garrett L. (aka GLOWZ), Founder
